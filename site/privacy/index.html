Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The honest summary: this site collects nothing. There is no account system, no comment form, no newsletter, no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies issued by this site. What follows is the plain-language detail, including the one category of data any website's infrastructure inevitably handles.

What we do not do

  • No cookies from this site. The site's own pages set no cookies. Your browser may receive connection-level technical artifacts from Cloudflare (see below), but no tracking, preference, or session cookies are placed by us.
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no heatmap tools, no fingerprinting scripts, no conversion trackers. We cannot tell who you are, where you came from, or what you clicked — and we don't want that data.
  • No advertising. No AdSense, no affiliate trackers, no sponsored scripts. This may change someday; if it ever does, this page will be rewritten first and the change noted at the top.
  • No third-party embeds. Pages load no YouTube embeds, no social widgets, no external fonts, no CDN-hosted JavaScript from third parties. Every script and stylesheet on the site is served from this domain.
  • No forms, no accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere to type your email. The machine-readable API (JSON/MCP) is read-only and requires no token.

What is handled anyway (infrastructure reality)

Any web server processes some technical data in order to serve a page at all. This site is served through Cloudflare, which acts as the connection layer. In the course of an ordinary visit, the following is necessarily processed, in ways we do not control and cannot identify you with:

  • Request logs. Your IP address, the page requested, the timestamp, and your browser's User-Agent string are processed by Cloudflare for security (blocking bot attacks) and briefly for delivery. These are infrastructure logs, not marketing data; this site has no dashboard of your visit and no access to identified profiles.
  • Security cookies. Cloudflare may set a small number of strictly technical cookies (for example cf_clearance during bot checks). These exist to keep the site reachable and are not used to profile you.

Cloudflare's own privacy policy governs that processing: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The content you read says nothing about you

Dream interpretation is personal — but reading this site leaves no trace that can be tied back to you. We do not know which symbol pages you visited, and no one else can find out from us. If you search "teeth falling out dream" and land here, that search lived in your search engine, not on this site.

Children

This site is general-audience reference content about classical texts. It is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU/UK), collects no data from anyone, and therefore collects none from children.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / and everywhere else)

Because this site holds no personal data about you — no names, no emails, no identifiers, no behavioral profiles — there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. If you believe otherwise, or if a regulation you are subject to requires a response from us anyway, contact us and we will answer within 30 days. The rights regimes (GDPR Articles 15–22, CCPA §1798.100 et seq.) attach to personal data; this site's design goal is for that category to be empty.

Changes

If the site ever adds analytics, advertising, forms, or accounts, this page will be updated before the change goes live, with the date noted at the top. The no-tracking design is a deliberate choice; it will not drift silently.
